### About Me

I am a second-year Ph.D. student at Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences (ICT, CAS), instructed by Prof. Xiaoming Sun. I received my bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Mathematics from University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS) in 2024. During my undergraduate studies, I was a visiting student at the University of California, Berkeley (UCB) in fall 2023. My research interests lie in quantum circuit synthesis and quantum machine learning, with publications in Quantum and ICML.
